What’s new in 0.2

More capable.
Still unmistakably small.

Float now handles the parts around the image with more care, without becoming another app you need to manage.

01

Settings stay off the image

Opacity, slideshow timing, aspect lock, and click-through live in a dedicated panel.

02

Every window keeps its place

Pin a mockup here and a cheat sheet there. Each viewer keeps its own image and navigation state.

03

Slideshows loop cleanly

Open an image set, choose the interval once, then move through it manually or automatically.

04

Quiet, clear viewer states

Empty, missing, and failed-load states explain what happened without filling the window with chrome.

Built for reference work

Made for the moment between looking and making.

Float is intentionally small: no dashboard, no sync model, no browser tabs. Just a viewer that keeps visual context alive while you draw, design, code, learn, or play.
